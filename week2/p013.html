Ceiling Fan Replacement During Renovation: A Project Checklist

TL;DR

  • Survey the ceiling support, wiring and wall controls before the ceiling is patched and painted.
  • Choose the exact fan before getting a final quote. Include access, taking down the old fan, support work and ceiling repairs.
  • For AirFi One, plan a handover that checks airflow, lighting and Bluetooth music, with app setup if you intend to use it.

Plan the new fan while the ceiling and wiring are still easy to reach. Survey the room, choose the model and agree the work before the final finish goes on. If the old box needs to be replaced, doing it now can avoid opening a freshly painted ceiling.

Survey before finishing the ceiling

Give the electrician the exact fan model and its manual. Ask them to check the support, power supply and wall controls. They also need to measure the space around the blades. For AirFi One, use the installation guide and hire a licensed electrician to fit the unit.

Include a room plan with new cabinets, doors and tall furniture. Measure to the finished floor and ceiling, allowing for any renovation changes. If the old fan will come down before the new one arrives, agree how the electrician will leave the connection safe during the gap.

Keep mounting work separate from decoration in the quote. Identify who opens the ceiling, who installs or replaces support, and who patches and paints it. The decorator needs to know when the electrician has finished work that would disturb the surface.

Get a quote for the whole replacement

A fixture price covers the product. Ask the quote to show removal of the old fan, support or box work, electrical changes, fitting the new unit and any return visit. List ceiling access and repairs separately so they are assigned to the right trade. Confirm any permits or inspections required for the actual work with the electrician.

AirFi One combines a Jamo-developed three-driver speaker system, a fan and independently controlled lighting at one ceiling point. Plan the listening area along with the fan location, leaving bedside tables free of speaker boxes. Mark the lamps you will keep and a place for the remote, so music and lighting controls are within reach.

Example: a bedroom renovation brief

In this example, an old fan is being replaced during a bedroom renovation. The ceiling will be repainted, a new wardrobe will be installed and the owner is considering AirFi One for music and airflow. The owner has a room sketch, photos of the existing fan and wall switch, and the AirFi One manual. The support box is still concealed, so the project needs a survey before a firm installation quote.

Test the functions you will use

For AirFi One, check the fan through its six speeds, direction control, white-light dimming and color settings, and RGB controls. Pair the owner’s phone over Bluetooth and play music. Turn the main light and fan off separately while music plays.

Set up the app if it is part of the job brief. Ask the installer to show how the wall switch works and what turns back on when power returns. Note any open issue before signing off the job.

Renovation questions

Can the decorator remove the old fan?

Have the electrician disconnect and remove it, and leave the point safe. The decorator can then plan the ceiling work around that visit.

What if the ceiling will be lowered?

Give the electrician the new ceiling height and ask them to check the space between the blades and the finished floor. The electrician also needs to know how the new ceiling affects access to the support and wiring.

How much time should I book for the replacement?

Ask the installer to estimate the time after the survey. A simple swap takes a different booking from a job that needs new support and ceiling repairs. Use the quote to plan the visit.
