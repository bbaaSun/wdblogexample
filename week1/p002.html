AirFi One’s $499 Ceiling Fan Speaker: What’s Included?

TL;DR

  • AirFi One’s $499 price includes a 48-inch flush-mount fan, white and RGB lighting, and a three-driver audio system developed with Jamo.
  • The speaker system has a 50 W woofer and two 15 W mid-high drivers, with DSP tuning and EQ presets.
  • The included remote controls music playback, lighting and fan settings. Installation is an additional cost.
  • Ceiling fan speakers below $200 also combine music, light and airflow. Compare the equipment included and the fit for your room before setting the budget.

AirFi One costs $499 for the complete fixture. Its audio system includes a dedicated bass driver, two mid-high drivers and adjustable sound. White and RGB lighting, a six-speed fan and a remote are part of the same purchase.

The fixture suits a room update where the ceiling point can serve music, lighting and airflow together. The installation quote sits on top of the purchase price, so room measurements and ceiling support belong in the budget from the start.

Inside the audio system

AirFi One’s audio system was developed with Jamo. The woofer handles bass, while the two mid-high drivers reproduce voices and higher-frequency instruments. Their combined rated output is 80 W RMS.

Driver size and layout describe how the system is built. The 5.25-inch woofer faces down into the room, while the smaller drivers face outward. Passive radiators support the bass response within the housing. DSP tuning shapes the system’s sound, and EQ lets you adjust the balance for your music.

The frequency range is 45 Hz–20 kHz at −10 dB with EQ enabled.

Sound adjustments you can use

The EQ presets include party and vocal-focused settings. They change the emphasis on bass and voices. Choose a setting with a familiar track at your usual listening volume. The remote then gives you volume, play/pause and track controls while your phone supplies the Bluetooth playlist.

When comparing this audio package with another fixture, look for the driver layout, sound adjustments and a demonstration at a similar volume. Bluetooth tells you how the music reaches the speaker; it says little about the speaker’s sound.

White lighting and color effects

The main light offers 3000K, 4000K and 6500K settings, with 10–100% dimming. Its output is 850 lumens with the shade fitted. Include that output alongside the room’s reading and task lights when planning illumination.

The RGB ring has separate controls and a music-reactive effect. You can dim the white light while using the color ring, or leave music playing with both lights off. The fan, main light, RGB ring and speaker operate independently.

Decide which parts of the package matter

AirFi One is a candidate when your shopping list includes a dedicated woofer, separate mid-high drivers and EQ in a flush-mount ceiling fixture. Those are concrete requirements you can compare against another model.

If your requirements are simply a fan, light and Bluetooth playback, the models below $200 already cover that combination. Spending more calls for an audio design, sound or control feature you specifically want. For a purchase that depends on sound quality, a listening demonstration is the most useful next step.

Allow for installation and future access

The $499 covers the fixture. An installer’s quote should include removal of the old fitting, suitable fan support, any wiring changes and controller setup. A room with an existing fan point may need different work from one with only a ceiling light.

AirFi One has a 13-inch overall height. The installation requirements call for at least 7 feet from blades to floor and 30 inches from blade tips to obstacles. Use the blade position in the mounting drawing for the floor-clearance calculation; the speaker and light sit below the blades.

The fan, light and speaker share the ceiling housing. Plan access to that location for service, and ask how removal and refitting would be charged. Add the installation quote to the fixture price before comparing total project costs.

Questions about the $499 package

Does the price include installation?

Installation is separate. The work depends on the ceiling support, wiring and fixture being replaced.

Does 80 W RMS include the fan and light?

The 80 W RMS rating is for audio: 50 W for the woofer and 15 W for each mid-high driver. The fan motor and lighting have separate power specifications.

Can I use Bluetooth music without setting up Wi-Fi?

Yes. Bluetooth carries music from your phone. Wi-Fi connects AirFi One to the app for setup and controls.
